About Navras

Nine emotions.
One cultural heartbeat.

NAVRAS is the official cultural society of JIMS Engineering Management Technical Campus (JEMTEC). Born from the idea of the nine "rasas" of Indian art, we celebrate music, dance, drama, art and every form of creative expression on campus.

Vision

To build a vibrant cultural ecosystem where every student finds their voice.

Mission

Curate world-class cultural experiences that inspire, connect and elevate.

Values

Creativity, inclusion, discipline and the joy of performing together.
